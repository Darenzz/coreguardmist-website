About Us

At CoreGuard, our objective is to empower homeowners with effective, non-lethal security solutions that provide peace of mind without the risks associated with firearms or other lethal weapons.

We believe that everyone deserves to feel safe in their own home. Our team of security experts and product developers has created CoreGuard Mist as an accessible solution to home protection that balances effectiveness with safety.

Our mission is to reduce home invasion crimes by providing homeowners with reliable self-defense tools backed by proper education on their use. We are committed to ongoing research and development to continuously improve our products while maintaining the highest standards of quality and safety.

CoreGuard products are manufactured in facilities that meet rigorous quality control standards, ensuring that when you need our products most, they perform exactly as expected.
